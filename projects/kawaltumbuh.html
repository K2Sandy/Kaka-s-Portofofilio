Overview

KawalTumbuh is an offline-first Android app that brings growth monitoring, nutrition guidance, and immunization tracking into one place to support stunting prevention. Stunting still affects 19.8% of Indonesian children under five (SSGI 2024), and at the household level it is often noticed late.

Parents often learn their child's nutritional status only at a monthly Posyandu check-up, MPASI menu advice rarely considers family budgets, and immunization schedules are easy to miss. KawalTumbuh puts four tools in the parent's hands: a Z-score child nutrition calculator, budget-adjusted MPASI recipes using local ingredients, an immunization schedule with automatic reminders, and nutrition guidance for expectant mothers.

Everything runs on the device, with no backend, so it works without internet and children's data never leaves the phone. Nutritional status is computed with the WHO LMS method and classified per Permenkes No. 2 of 2020, and recipe ranking adapts energy and protein targets to the child's status.

Testing covered eight black-box scenarios (all passed), Z-scores verified against WHO tables (maximum difference 0.01 SD, well under the 0.1 SD tolerance), and a usability test with 30 parents of children under five that averaged 4.65 out of 5 (93.00%, Very Good). It is a prototype: the results measure user acceptance, not any effect on stunting cases.
